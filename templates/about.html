ABOUT KAAMPAKKA

Local repairs without
surprise charges.

KaamPakka is an AI-guided repair-request desk for the gap between “something is broken” and “a technician is at the door.” It makes the job, quote, and booking terms clear before payment.

01

Structure

AI turns a blurry photo, invoice, or vague message into a technician-ready repair brief.

02

Guard

The buyer sees visit fee, parts cap, arrival window, and warranty terms before committing.

03

Book

PayPal Sandbox starts a booking deposit only after the buyer approves the selected technician.
