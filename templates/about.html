ABOUT THE PRODUCT

A calmer way to
navigate the cost of care.

Nivara is an AI care-commerce experience for people who need to understand a medical bill, see credible lower-cost options, and decide with confidence before paying.

01

Understand

OCR and AI convert dense bill data into plain-language, reviewable evidence.

02

Choose

Users see price intelligence and retain full control over every care-plan decision.

03

Pay

PayPal Sandbox checkout turns an approved plan into a traceable payment event.

HOW IT WORKS

Not a bill scanner.
A decision layer for care.

  1. 01
    Bring the evidence

    Upload a bill or prescription and review extracted items.

  2. 02
    Let the agent prepare a plan

    Nivara surfaces high-confidence lower-cost alternatives with transparent savings.

  3. 03
    Approve before checkout

    The user acknowledges the plan before a PayPal payment can begin.
